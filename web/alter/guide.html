The panel
0

The FM-1 as ALTER uses it. A page button tapped opens its quick page (the four knobs change with it); held 0.5 s opens its menu. HOME tapped always brings you back to the three circles.

The keys
I

Two rows: the 11 upper keys (the "black" ones) and the 16 lower ones (the "white" ones). On the circles' view (no menu open) they play ALTER, not notes.

The dice
X

The three DICE keys (lower row, keys 5 6 7) each draw for their own circle. What a die draws depends on what you hold with it.

Song
XX

Three slots A B C keep the rhythms of the three circles (steps, lanes, ranges, the dice's draw, REC's moves, the mutes). The sounds stay as they are. A slot always starts at the next bar, every circle from its step 1. What you change while a slot plays stays in it (until power-off; SAVE held + its key keeps it for good).

On the screen

Save & names
XXI

SAVE opens the SAVE page: 3 tabs (ALGORITHM) — SCENES A, SCENES B, SCENES C — of 16 slots each (48 scenes). A scene is everything (sounds, rhythms, effects, REC, ranges, what the dice, CHAOS and CHAOS TOTAL drew, the chord PROG; a TURING comes back frozen as it was). For variations of the rhythms, use SONG's slots A B C.

MIDI learn
XIX

Any MIDI controller (USB or TRS) can drive ALTER. HOME held opens SYSTEM; ALGORITHM to the LEARN tab. Its first row, GROUP (KNOB 1), picks what the list shows. SELECT a row, turn KNOB 1 right: it says WAITING — move a knob or press a pad on your controller: linked. KNOB 1 left clears the row's link.
