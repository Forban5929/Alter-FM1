Plug your FM-1 in over USB, switch it on with ALTER, then click “Connect the FM-1”. Chrome or Edge will ask to let the page exchange MIDI messages with the synth: allow it.

  1. What you move here changes the sound on the synth at once, and what you turn on the synth moves here.
  2. “Save” keeps the three sounds in a file on your computer, to share with anyone.
  3. “Store here” keeps the whole scene (sounds, rhythms, effects) in a USER slot of the synth, like holding SAVE.

No synth at hand? Open the web emulator in another tab, switch it on, then click “Connect the emulator”: you hear it, and its screen and this page follow each other. “Try without a synth” runs ALTER inside this page, without sound.

CHAOS: a new draw on the three circles. CHAOS TOTAL: everything at random; pressed again within 3 seconds, it is undone.

ALTER is free firmware (GPL-3.0) built on Felucca. Code: github.com/Forban5929/Alter-FM1. Fonts: TeX Gyre Pagella (GUST Font License), Inter Tight and IBM Plex Mono (SIL Open Font License).
